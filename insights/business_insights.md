# Business Insights

## Dataset
Synthetic customer and sales dataset created for learning and demonstration purposes.

## Key Insights

- Total sales are 129K across 8 orders.
- Total profit is 22K with an overall profit margin of 17.29%.
- Average Order Value is 16.13K.
- Electronics is the highest-selling category.
- South Asia contributes the highest regional sales.
- Laptop is the highest-selling product.
- Aarav Sharma is the highest-value customer by sales.
- January generated higher sales and profit than February.
- Office Supplies has the highest profit margin among the categories.

## Data Quality

- 8 records validated.
- No missing values identified.
- No duplicate Order IDs identified.
- Customer and product mappings were validated.
- Country and region mappings were validated.
- Quantity, sales, profit, and discount values passed validation checks.
